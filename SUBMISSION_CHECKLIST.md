## Selected track

**Best Apps and Agents.** ProofDesk helps hackathon builders compare requirements against repository evidence and prepare an honest submission. Its core assessment uses Nemotron on Token Factory. Hosting the UI on Nebius is optional under the supplied rules because a real Token Factory inference call satisfies the runtime definition. A localhost address alone is not a judge-accessible submission URL.

## Judging focus

Implementation, design, potential impact, and quality of idea are equally weighted. Demonstrate a useful evidence-to-action workflow and a credible real audience. The rules do not guarantee that meeting the checklist makes a project competitive or eligible.

Finalize submission materials before the deadline. The supplied rules restrict changes to the hackathon submission after the submission period, except modifications specifically permitted by the organizers.
